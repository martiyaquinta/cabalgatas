Horseback Ride Program

Day 1 - Holy Thursday

We welcome you at El Montañés from 11:00 am to 1:00 pm. Briefing and guide introductions. Packing saddlebags. Lunch.

3:00 pm: Departure from Portezuelo Ancho. We ride to Valle Hermoso (3 to 3.5 hours of riding).

After mid-afternoon, we prepare the “beds” with tarps, part of the saddle, sleeping bag and blankets. At the same time the guide’s assistant prepares the fire for cooking. Night under the stars, campfire and guitar, and the silence of the Andes.

Day 2 - Good Friday

8:00 am: Breakfast, we saddle the horses. We ride through the great valley of Las Vegas Negras.

Lunch is a cold packed meal.

In the afternoon we reach 3,000 m a.s.l. at the “Three Valleys Viewpoint”, a paradise spot. We can see the Río Cobre Valley, Matansilla Valley and Valle Hermoso.

We return to camp where delicious fried cakes and mate await us. Second night in the field. Dinner.

Day 3 - Saturday

8:00 am: Breakfast. We keep enjoying the beautiful surrounding valley, cross the Río Tordillo and pass near Laguna Valle Hermoso.

Lunch.

We head to Río Cobre for a thermal bath in the middle of the mountains.

Return to base camp. Mid-afternoon. Dinner.

Day 4 - Sunday

Last half day of riding. We say goodbye to our trail companions.

Return to El Montañés by transfer from the Valle Hermoso Viewpoint.

Lunch, with the option of a refreshing bath.

End of service: 3:00 pm.

Riding times may vary according to the guides’ decisions. The program may change depending on weather and river conditions.

WHAT SHOULD I BRING?

  • Sleeping bag rated for -10°C / 14°F
  • Hat with chin strap (so it doesn’t blow away in the wind)
  • Rain poncho / rain gear
  • Two changes of clothes. We recommend field bombachas or loose pants. Prefer shirts over crew-neck t-shirts for better sun protection. Bring several thin layers rather than one very thick coat. Fleece jackets work well in any season
  • A warm undershirt, leggings or long underwear
  • Warm jacket and/or poncho
  • Wool hat
  • Neck scarf, scarf and gardening gloves
  • Swimsuit and shorts
  • Sneakers and espadrilles. Very wide footwear (e.g. heavy boots) is not ideal for riding — it can get caught in the stirrup. Experienced riders may use riding boots. Sneakers should be waterproof
  • Personal medication
  • Insect repellent
  • No vaccines are mandatory in the area. Still, as with any outdoor activity, we suggest a tetanus shot
  • Personal hygiene items (wet wipes welcome). Two towels
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Sunglasses with a retainer strap (optional)
  • Headlamp
  • Binoculars (optional)
  • Mug, plate and cutlery
  • Camera
